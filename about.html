Kevin's Background

Why I Started AudienceIntent

I started AudienceIntent because I'd spent too long watching business owners put their trust in people who didn't take that responsibility seriously. Spam passed off as marketing. Fees nobody could explain. Subscriptions that kept billing long after someone had forgotten what they were paying for.

What bothered me was how normal it had become. Behind every invoice was someone trying to build a business, pay their team, and make a living. They deserved better. I wanted to build a company where they could feel comfortable asking questions and expect an honest answer.

One thing I've never been comfortable with is agencies earning a percentage of what a client spends on advertising. The agency makes more when the client spends more, whether that spending helps or not. I've watched businesses get pushed to increase budgets without a clear reason beyond the extra fees it would generate. I couldn't build a business around that.

For Database Reactivation, we only get paid from the revenue we generate for you. If we don't produce revenue, we don't get paid. I like knowing we're working towards the same outcome.

AI Recommended involves a different kind of commitment. Helping your business get found and trusted by AI takes ongoing work across your content, digital presence, technical setup, and reporting. We charge a fixed price for that work. Whether your business makes $100,000 a year or $10 million, the price is the same. Your success shouldn't be a reason to charge you more for the same service.

I don't expect anyone to trust us because of something I've written on a website. We have to earn that through the work, the conversations, and how we handle things when they don't go to plan. But you should always know what you're paying for, what we're doing, and where things stand.

That's the company I wanted to work with. So that's the company I'm building.
