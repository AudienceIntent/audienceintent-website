The Process

The 6 steps

What actually happens once you start, from the first week to ongoing maintenance.

1

Technical setup

Days 1–3

If AI can't read your site, you're invisible. We make sure AI can actually find and read your website before anything else starts.

78% of brands are completely absent from AI answers.
  • Review and correct llms.txt and robots.txt — created if they don't exist — so AI can access every page
  • Check and submit your XML sitemap so every page on your site can be found
  • Add Organization, WebPage, FAQ, and Article schema so AI can understand your content
  • Analyze site speed and any technical issues blocking AI
  • Install reporting and tracking so you can see results from day one
  • Unblock any AI crawlers currently being blocked, then confirm access across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok
2

Baseline & prompt library

Week 1

We establish exactly where you stand today, so every step after this has a clear target to build toward.

Only ~12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10 — AI visibility and search rankings aren't the same game.
  • Audit your current standing across every major AI model — so we know exactly where you're starting
  • Build a full prompt library — branded prompts that name you, and unbranded prompts people ask without knowing you exist
  • Review sentiment — what AI engines are actually saying about you, positive and negative
  • That library becomes the target for every piece of content we create
3

AI-first content

Live Week 1

Content written for AI to read and cite — not written to rank on a search results page.

More buyers are asking AI directly instead of typing a search query — if your content doesn't answer them, a competitor's does.
  • Each piece answers the exact questions your potential customers are asking
  • Every piece is fully AI-optimized with schema markup
  • Minimum two new pieces of content published every week, so your library keeps growing
4

Reputation, citations & traffic sources

First 30 days

What's said about you elsewhere often matters more than what's on your own site — this is where your trust signals get built.

85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party sources, not the brand's own website. Digital PR, reviews, and community mentions drive AI visibility.
  • Clean up your Google Business Profile and social profiles — mismatched names, addresses, and phone numbers
  • Target where your competitors are currently getting mentioned, and aim to get you listed there instead
  • Track which sites and sources are sending you AI traffic, so we know exactly where to focus next
  • Track every mention and citation, so you know exactly how often you show up
  • Point you to the Digital PR placements worth pursuing, based on real metrics
  • Combat negative sentiment with factual content, case studies, and real use cases

What to expect: citations move first, typically within 4–8 weeks, with traffic following around 60–90 days after.

5

Ongoing technical health

Monthly

AI systems favor sites that stay current, so this step never really stops.

3x more likely to be cited: content updated within the last 90 days, compared to older content.
  • Keep checking that AI can still see and read your site
  • Refresh content every 30–60 days to keep it relevant to AI
  • Monthly optimization audit — specific changes to your site and content pages, ready for your team to implement
6

Tracking & reporting

Weekly · Monthly · Quarterly

You should always know exactly where you stand.

Every report is written in plain language — no technical jargon.
  • Weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting
  • Your visibility score and how much traffic is coming directly from AI
  • We track you against your direct competitors — real numbers that show what's actually working, not jargon
  • Reporting is customizable — tell us what you want to see, and we'll add it to your report

↻ Ongoing — runs for as long as you're a client
