It means making sure tools like ChatGPT, Google AI and Perplexity can find your business, understand what you do, and recommend you when someone asks for help. More people now ask a question instead of scrolling through links. AEO is how you become one of the names in the answer.

They work together. SEO helps you rank in a list of links. AEO helps you get named in the answer. These tools still lean on search results, so a healthy website matters. But they also look at your reviews, what other websites say about you, and whether your content answers real questions clearly. We cover all of it.

They pull from your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews, directories, articles and forums. They look for businesses that are clear about what they do, consistent everywhere, and talked about by sources they trust. The more places that back you up, the more likely you are to be named.
