SEO helps your business appear in search results; AEO helps it appear in AI answers and recommendations. You need both because ranking first on Google does not guarantee that ChatGPT, Google AI, or Perplexity will recommend you. AEO should work alongside your SEO—not replace the work or the people already helping you rank.

## AEO vs SEO: What Is the Difference?

Search engine optimization, or SEO, improves your visibility in search results. Answer engine optimization, or AEO, makes your business and content easier for AI systems to find, understand, cite, and recommend.

The simplest distinction is **search rankings versus AI answers**. The work overlaps, but the outcome you measure is different.

| Question | SEO | AEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is the main goal? | Earn visibility and clicks from search results | Earn accurate mentions, citations, and recommendations in AI answers |
| Where does it show up? | Google and Bing results, including local search | ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Perplexity |
| What does the customer do? | Searches, compares results, and may click | Asks a question, reads an answer, and may research further or contact a business |
| What work matters? | Technical health, useful pages, internal links, and credible outside references | The same foundations, plus clear business facts, direct answers, and AI-specific testing |
| What should you measure? | Search impressions, rankings, clicks, leads, and sales | Mentions, citations, recommendations, share of voice, referral traffic, and leads |
| Does success guarantee the other? | No. Strong rankings do not guarantee AI recommendations | No. An AI mention does not guarantee strong search rankings |

These are not completely separate worlds. Google itself combines traditional results with AI answers, and AI systems can use web search to find sources. That is why treating AEO as unrelated to SEO is just as misleading as treating them as identical.

For the wider definition, see [what AEO and GEO are](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/what-is-answer-engine-optimization-aeo-the-complete-guide-for-2026).

## What SEO Still Does Well

SEO still helps customers find your services, products, locations, and answers through search. The arrival of AI answers changes where attention goes; it does not make your website or search presence unnecessary.

Google handles [more than 5 trillion searches a year](https://searchengineland.com/google-5-trillion-searches-per-year-452928), according to Google in 2025. That is roughly 14 billion searches a day.

People still need to compare providers, check prices, confirm service areas, read product details, and make purchases. Strong service pages, accurate Google Business Profiles, useful articles, and healthy websites support those decisions.

Keep tracking which pages bring qualified visitors and customers. Do not assume that all informational traffic has stopped working, or that commercial and local searches are untouched by AI. Look at your own results by query type and page.

### Good SEO Gives AEO a Head Start

The technical and content work behind good SEO also helps AI systems discover and interpret your business. You should build on that work rather than pay to repeat it.

The shared foundations include:

- **Accessible pages:** Important content should be available to relevant search and AI crawlers.
- **Clear site structure:** Descriptive headings and internal links help systems find related information.
- **Useful content:** Accurate, specific explanations give both customers and AI systems something worth reading.
- **Credible references:** Relevant links and outside mentions can help establish context and trust.
- **Consistent business facts:** Your name, services, location, and contact information should agree across major profiles.

Schema can help describe your business and pages, but it is not a ranking or recommendation guarantee. Likewise, a third-party domain authority score is not a universal admission requirement for AI visibility.

**If your SEO is solid, you are not starting from zero with AEO.** You are adding a goal, a set of tests, and work aimed at AI answers.

## Why You Can Rank First on Google and Still Be Invisible in AI

A Google ranking tells you where a page appears for a particular search. It does not tell you whether an AI system will select that page, describe your business accurately, or recommend you for a customer's specific situation.

Consider a roofing company that ranks first for “roof repair Wellington.” A homeowner asks an AI assistant: “Who repairs tile roofs in Wellington and offers emergency help?”

The company's ranking is useful. But its website may never clearly explain that it repairs tile roofs or offers emergency service. Another business may have a page that answers those questions directly, supported by consistent business information elsewhere.

The first business can win the search ranking and still miss the recommendation. That is an example of how the gap can happen—not a rule about which company an AI will choose.

### What AEO Adds

AEO makes the information needed for a recommendation easier to find and verify. It also checks whether that information is actually showing up in answers.

Useful work includes:

1. **Researching customer questions.** Cover real needs, service areas, costs, limitations, comparisons, and buying decisions—not just short keywords.
2. **Stating business facts clearly.** Explain what you do, whom you serve, and where you operate.
3. **Writing direct answers.** Open a section with the answer, then add details and evidence.
4. **Keeping important facts consistent.** Small name variations do not automatically create separate businesses, but conflicting addresses, phone numbers, or service descriptions can create confusion.
5. **Checking outside information.** AI systems may consult business profiles, publications, reviews, and other sources. Your website is not the only place customers or AI can learn about you.
6. **Testing AI responses over time.** Check whether the right business appears, whether the description is accurate, and which sources are cited.

Do not confuse a citation with a recommendation. An AI can cite an article from your website without recommending your company as a provider.

## Why Add AEO Now?

AI answers are a large enough discovery channel to deserve their own attention. That is a reason to expand your visibility work, not abandon search.

OpenAI reported that ChatGPT had **about 1.2 billion weekly active users in November 2026**, according to [Big Technology](https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/openai-chatgpt-search-is-about-20). Weekly users are not the same as searches or purchase-ready customers, but the scale matters.

AI summaries also change clicking behavior. [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org) found that users clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, compared with 15% when one did not.

A separate [Seer Interactive study](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update) reported organic click-through rates falling from 1.76% to 0.61% for the AI Overview query group it studied—a decline of about 61%.

Those findings describe particular studies, not a guaranteed traffic loss for every business. They do explain why a rankings-only report can miss a change in how customers discover providers.

The response is to measure two things: whether customers can find your pages, and whether AI answers include your business.

## How Your SEO Provider and AEO Team Should Work Together

Keep your SEO provider if the work is good. Add AI-specific responsibilities to the existing scope or bring in help for the parts the current team does not handle.

Some SEO providers already do substantial AEO work. Others focus on rankings and organic traffic. Ask about deliverables rather than assuming the label tells you everything.

A useful conversation starts with these questions:

- Which customer questions are we testing in AI answers?
- Are we tracking business recommendations as well as website citations?
- Who fixes inaccurate business information?
- Who owns schema, crawler access, and page edits?
- How will we report search results and AI visibility separately?

Use one shared content calendar and one owner for each technical change. If both teams edit a service page independently, you risk duplicate work or conflicting changes.

For example, the SEO team might identify a valuable service query. The AEO team can add related customer questions and check whether AI answers name the business. One improved page can serve both goals.

**AEO is not a threat to your SEO provider. It is another outcome the work should support.**

## How to Split Your SEO and AEO Budget

Protect the SEO work that brings customers, then fund the gaps in your AI visibility. There is no universal budget split based on business size or a domain authority score.

Start by identifying the actual problem. A blocked website needs technical repairs. A strong website with weak AI visibility needs clearer answers, accurate business facts, and consistent testing. Those are different spending decisions.

### Illustrative Starting Splits

The percentages below are planning examples for SEO and AEO spending combined—not research benchmarks or percentages of your entire marketing budget. Adjust them to your costs and results.

| Your situation | Example SEO/AEO split | First priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Important pages are missing or difficult to crawl | 75% / 25% | Repair the foundation while establishing an AI baseline |
| SEO brings steady leads, but AI rarely names you | 60% / 40% | Maintain useful SEO work and address AI-specific gaps |
| Search performance is strong and competitors dominate relevant AI answers | 50% / 50% | Test a larger AEO effort without neglecting search |

Shared work should be counted once. A schema repair or an updated service page may support both SEO and AEO; it does not need two separate budgets.

You can use the same approach for staff time. Decide which tasks matter most, assign an owner, and agree on what will be delivered over the next 90 days.

### Before You Move Money, Check Four Things

Make a budget change only after you understand what you are protecting and what you want to improve.

1. **Current customers:** Which search pages produce qualified inquiries or sales?
2. **Technical problems:** Can relevant crawlers access your important pages?
3. **AI gaps:** Which customer questions name competitors but not you?
4. **Available capacity:** Can your team publish, update, and measure consistently?

You do not need perfect SEO before starting AEO. Fix serious problems first, but basic AI visibility checks and clearer business information can begin immediately.

Review the split after 90 days. Keep spending tied to useful work and observed progress, not a fixed percentage that never changes.

## How to Measure SEO and AEO Separately

Measure SEO by search discovery and business outcomes. Measure AEO by what appears in AI answers, then connect that visibility to traffic and inquiries where possible.

A single “organic traffic” number cannot explain both.

| Measure | What to track | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO visibility | Search impressions and rankings for important queries | Whether your pages appear in search |
| SEO visits | Clicks, click-through rate, and organic sessions | Whether search visibility leads to visits |
| SEO outcomes | Qualified inquiries, sales, and conversion rate | Whether those visits help the business |
| AI mentions | Answers that name your business | Whether AI includes you |
| AI citations | Answers that link to your website or another source about you | Which sources support the answer |
| AI recommendations | Answers that suggest your business for the customer's need | Whether you are being presented as a provider to consider |
| AI share of voice | Your share of tracked business mentions compared with competitors | How much of the measured conversation you receive |
| AI outcomes | Identifiable referral visits, inquiries, and sales | What can be connected to AI discovery |

### Make the AI Test Repeatable

Use a stable set of customer questions and record the platform, date, location assumptions, and results. AI answers can change, so one favorable screenshot is not a trend.

Track discovery questions, comparisons, service-area questions, and specific customer needs. Do not test only prompts that already contain your business name.

Define each metric before reporting it. For example, share of voice can mean your business's mentions divided by all tracked business mentions. That is different from the percentage of answers containing your name. Keep the same definition and competitor set over time.

### Be Honest About Attribution

AI discovery does not always produce a trackable click. Someone may read a recommendation, search your name later, and call.

GA4 can identify some visits from known AI referrers, but it will not reveal every AI-assisted customer journey. Google AI visits may also appear within Google organic reporting rather than as a separate AI source.

Report identifiable AI referrals, search performance, and customer-reported discovery separately. Do not add overlapping totals together or assume every branded search came from AI.

For a fuller measurement plan, see [how to measure AEO campaign success](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-to-measure-the-success-of-an-aeo-campaign-metrics-kpis-and-benchmarks-for-2026).

## What to Do in the First 90 Days

Use the first 90 days to establish a baseline, fix important gaps, and repeat the tests. Do not treat it as a deadline for a guaranteed recommendation.

### First Month: Establish the Facts

Check your current search performance and AI visibility. Confirm crawler access, important business details, service pages, Google Business Profile information, and the customer questions your content should answer.

### Second Month: Publish and Improve

Add useful answers where information is missing. Update older pages, correct conflicting facts, and coordinate changes with your SEO provider. Check which pages AI systems cite and whether the business descriptions are accurate.

### Third Month: Review the Pattern

Compare repeated tests against the baseline. Look for relevant mentions, accurate recommendations, citations, competitor changes, and identifiable inquiries—not just a larger count of low-value mentions.

**Perplexity can pick up a new page within a couple of weeks. ChatGPT and Google AI usually take two to three months to recommend a business on a steady basis.** Results vary, and nobody can guarantee an appearance in a specific AI answer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is AEO just SEO with a new name?

No. SEO focuses on visibility in search results, while AEO focuses on visibility in AI answers and recommendations. They share technical and content foundations, but AEO adds AI-specific question research, response testing, and measurement. Good work can support both without making them identical.

### Do I need to replace my SEO provider to do AEO?

No. Keep a provider that does good work. Ask which AI-specific tasks and measurements are already included, then assign any gaps. Coordinate page edits, technical changes, and content plans so the teams do not duplicate work.

### Can I rank first on Google and still be invisible in AI?

Yes. Ranking first for a search does not guarantee that an AI system will select your page or recommend your business for a related question. AI visibility depends on the question, available sources, business information, and the answer generated at that time.

### How much should I spend on AEO versus SEO?

There is no universal split. Protect SEO work that brings qualified customers, fix serious website problems, and fund the AI visibility gaps you can identify. Set a 90-day plan, count shared work once, and adjust spending based on results rather than a domain authority threshold.

### How long does AEO take to work?

Perplexity can pick up a new page within a couple of weeks. ChatGPT and Google AI usually take two to three months to recommend a business on a steady basis. That is why AudienceIntent starts with 90 days. No provider can guarantee inclusion in a specific answer.

### How do I measure AEO if people do not click?

Track business mentions, citations, recommendations, and share of voice across a consistent set of customer questions. Add identifiable AI referral traffic, inquiries, and customer-reported discovery. Report these separately because an AI mention is not a website visit, and not every AI-assisted sale is traceable.

### Do local businesses need both SEO and AEO?

Yes. Local SEO helps customers find your business through search and maps. AEO helps make your services, location, and suitability clear in AI answers. Accurate business facts, useful service pages, and a well-maintained Google Business Profile support both, without guaranteeing a recommendation.
