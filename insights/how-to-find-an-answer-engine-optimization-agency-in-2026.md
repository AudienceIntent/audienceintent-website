# How to Find an Answer Engine Optimization Agency in 2026

Most businesses that rank well in Google are invisible in AI search. A customer opens ChatGPT, types "best \[your service\] near me" or "who should I hire for \[your category\]," and gets a confident, cited answer — one that doesn't include you. Your competitor shows up. You don't.

That gap is what an answer engine optimization agency is built to close.

**The core problem:** Only 12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10. Traditional SEO and AI visibility are separate ecosystems, and most SEO agencies aren't equipped to work in both.

This guide explains what AEO agencies do, when it makes sense to hire one, what separates a capable agency from one that's just rebranded its SEO deck, and what questions to ask before signing anything. If you're comparing options or starting from scratch, this is the framework to use.

## What Is an Answer Engine Optimization Agency?

An answer engine optimization agency helps businesses get cited in AI-generated answers — the responses produced by platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini when someone asks a question.

This is different from traditional SEO. SEO agencies work to improve your ranking in Google's blue-link results. An AEO agency works to improve your visibility in the answers AI platforms generate when users ask conversational questions, comparison queries, or "best of" prompts. Those answers pull from a different set of signals: third-party sources, structured content, citation patterns, and how well a business's information is understood by AI crawlers.

> **Key distinction:** AI search and Google search are not the same channel. A business can rank on page one of Google and still be completely absent from every AI-generated answer in its category.

The best AEO agencies understand both ecosystems. They know which signals influence AI citations, how to track visibility across multiple platforms, and how to close the gap between where a business appears and where its customers are now searching.

### Which platforms does AEO cover?

The major AI answer platforms that AEO agencies should be tracking include:

-   **ChatGPT** (OpenAI): The most widely used AI assistant, now handling over a billion queries per week
    
-   **Perplexity:** A search-native AI platform that cites sources directly and is growing fast among research-oriented users
    
-   **Google AI Overviews:** Google's AI-generated summaries that appear above organic results, [reducing organic click-through rates by 61%](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-impact-on-ctr/512345/) for many queries
    
-   **Gemini:** Google's standalone AI assistant, separate from AI Overviews but drawing from similar signals
    
-   **Microsoft Copilot:** Bing-powered AI that surfaces business recommendations in productivity and search contextsd search contexts
    

Any agency that only tracks one or two of these is giving you an incomplete picture.

## What Do Answer Engine Optimization Agencies Do?

AEO agencies work across three areas: measuring where you stand, improving the signals that drive citations, and reporting on changes over time. Here's what that looks like in practice.

### Measurement and tracking

Before anything else, a credible AEO agency needs to establish a baseline. That means running an AI visibility audit to document where your brand appears, where it's absent, and what your competitors are being cited for instead.

Specific activities include:

-   **AI visibility audits:** Systematically testing prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini to document your current citation footprint
    
-   **Prompt and query tracking:** Identifying which questions and search phrases trigger AI answers in your category, and whether your brand appears in those answers
    
-   **Competitor visibility analysis:** Benchmarking your AI presence against named competitors to identify where they're winning citations you should be capturing
    
-   **Citation tracking:** Distinguishing between brand mentions (your name appears in an answer) and citations (an AI links to or directly credits your content as a source) as a source)
    

The mention vs. citation distinction matters. A mention with no citation does less for your authority than a direct citation. Agencies that conflate the two are measuring the wrong thing.

### Content and technical optimization

Once the baseline is set, the work shifts to improving the signals that AI platforms use when deciding what to cite.

-   **Content optimization for direct answers:** Restructuring or creating content so it answers specific questions clearly and completely, in a format AI platforms can extract and cite
    
-   **Technical site improvements:** Ensuring AI crawlers can access, parse, and understand your site (schema markup, page structure, crawlability)
    
-   **Third-party source optimization:** Building and improving your presence on the directories, review platforms, and external sources that AI platforms weight heavily; [85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party sources](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-to-make-your-business-visible-to-ai-search-a-step-by-step-guide/), not your own websiten website
    

### Reporting

Visibility without measurement is guesswork. The reporting layer should show:

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Share of voice | How often your brand appears vs. competitors across tracked prompts |
| Citation count | How many times AI platforms link to your content as a source |
| Mention rate | How often your brand name appears in AI answers (with or without a citation) |
| Average position | Where in an AI answer your brand typically appears (first mention vs. buried) |
| Prompt-level breakdown | Which specific queries you're winning or missing |

If an agency can't show you this data broken down by platform and prompt, they're not doing AEO — they're doing SEO with a new name on it.

## When Should You Hire an AEO Agency?

AEO is still an emerging discipline, and not every business is ready for it on the same timeline. That said, several clear signals indicate the timing is right.

**You should consider hiring an AEO agency if any of these apply:**

-   Your competitors appear in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers for your category, but your business does not
    
-   Google AI Overviews cite a competitor or a third-party review site instead of you, even for queries where you rank organically
    
-   Your website performs well in traditional search but generates no measurable traffic from AI platforms
    
-   You have no idea which prompts mention your brand, which ones miss you, or what AI platforms say about your category
    
-   You operate in a service category where customers are increasingly using conversational AI to find providers (home services, legal, health, financial, professional services, and most local categories)
    
-   You want to track AI visibility over time, not just run a one-time check
    

**The SEO-ranking-but-invisible problem is more common than most businesses realize.** AI platforms don't simply pull from Google's top results. They build answers from a different set of sources: structured content, third-party citations, review platforms, directories, and pages that answer questions directly. A business can hold a top-three Google ranking and still not appear in a single AI-generated answer in its category.

If you've noticed a decline in organic click-through rates since Google AI Overviews expanded — a trend that has [reduced CTR for many high-ranking pages](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/) — that's a direct signal that AI search is already affecting your traffic, whether you're tracking it or not.

The businesses that move on this now have an advantage. AI platforms develop citation habits over time: once a competitor becomes the default recommended source in your category, displacing them takes longer than establishing your presence before they do.

## What to Look for in an Answer Engine Optimization Agency

The AEO space is new enough that almost any agency can claim expertise without much scrutiny. These are the criteria that separate agencies doing real work from those that have added "AI search" to their service list without changing anything underneath.

### Multi-platform tracking

AI visibility isn't a single number. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini each have different citation patterns, different source preferences, and different answer formats. An agency that only monitors one platform is giving you a partial view. The best AEO agencies track all major platforms and report on each one separately.

### Citations vs. Mentions: Why the Difference Matters

A mention means your brand name appeared somewhere in an AI answer. A citation means an AI platform pointed to your content as a source. Both matter, but they're not equivalent. If an agency reports "mentions" without breaking out citations, they may be inflating your apparent visibility. Ask specifically: how many of those mentions included a direct citation or link?

### Competitive share of voice

You need to know how your AI visibility compares to your direct competitors, not just your own trend line. A good AEO agency tracks share of voice: out of all the prompts in your category, what percentage produce an answer that includes your brand vs. a competitor? This is the metric that tells you whether you're winning or losing ground.

### Page-level citation data

Knowing that AI platforms cite your website is useful. Knowing which specific pages they cite — and which ones they ignore — is actionable. Agencies that can identify citation sources at the page level give you something to work with: you can see what's working, replicate it, and fix what isn't.

### Content built for direct answers

AI platforms favor content that answers questions directly, completely, and in a format they can extract. An agency should be able to audit your existing content against that standard and produce or recommend new content that meets it. If their content work is indistinguishable from standard blog production, it's not optimized for AI citation.

### Third-party trust signals

Because [85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party sources](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-to-make-your-business-visible-to-ai-search-a-step-by-step-guide/) rather than a business's own website, an AEO agency needs to work outside your domain. That means building and maintaining your presence on review platforms, directories, industry publications, and other sources that AI platforms treat as authoritative.

### Before-and-after reporting

Any agency worth hiring should be able to show you where you started and where you are now, in measurable terms. That means documented baselines, tracked prompt sets, and reporting that shows directional change over time. If they can't demonstrate progress with data, you have no way to evaluate whether the work is producing results.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring an AEO Agency

Use these questions in any sales conversation with a prospective AEO agency. The answers will tell you quickly whether they have real capability or are working from a general SEO playbook with updated terminology.

1.  **Which AI platforms do you track?** The answer should include ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini at minimum. "We monitor AI search" is not a sufficient answer.
2.  **Can you show me where my brand is mentioned or missed right now?** A credible agency can pull this data before you sign anything. If they need a retainer to run an initial audit, that's a red flag — basic visibility data should be demonstrable upfront.
3.  **Do you track competitor visibility?** Your AI presence only matters in context. An agency that doesn't benchmark against your competitors can't tell you whether you're gaining or losing ground.
4.  **Do you measure citations separately from mentions?** This is a filtering question. Agencies that treat mentions and citations as the same metric don't understand the distinction — or are deliberately obscuring it.
5.  **Can you identify which specific pages AI platforms are citing?** Page-level data is what makes optimization actionable. If they can only report at the domain level, their insights will be too broad to act on.
6.  **How do you improve visibility over time?** Look for a concrete answer: content creation, third-party source building, technical fixes, prompt set expansion. "We optimize for AI" is not a methodology.
7.  **Do you provide prompt-level reporting?** You should be able to see exactly which queries your brand appears in and which ones it doesn't. Aggregate scores without prompt-level detail hide more than they reveal.
8.  **What does success look like at 30, 60, and 90 days?** AEO is a compounding strategy. An agency should be able to describe realistic early indicators (citation baseline established, new sources indexed) and what meaningful progress looks like over a longer horizon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an answer engine optimization agency?

An answer engine optimization agency helps businesses appear in AI-generated answers on platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini. Unlike traditional SEO agencies, which focus on Google rankings, AEO agencies work to improve citation rates, share of voice, and brand mentions in AI search results.

### How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO improves your ranking in Google's blue-link results. AEO improves your visibility in AI-generated answers. The two channels use different signals: Google ranks pages based on backlinks, authority, and on-page optimization; AI platforms cite sources based on content structure, third-party credibility, and how directly content answers specific questions. Only 12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10, which means strong SEO performance does not translate automatically to AI visibility.

### Can an AEO agency help my business show up in ChatGPT?

An AEO agency can improve the signals that influence whether ChatGPT cites your business — including content structure, third-party source presence, and citation patterns. No agency can guarantee placement in a specific ChatGPT answer, because AI platforms make those decisions independently. What a credible agency can do is document your current visibility, identify what's missing, and systematically improve the factors that drive citations.

### What should I look for in an AEO agency?

Look for multi-platform tracking (not just one AI tool), a clear distinction between citations and mentions, competitive share of voice reporting, page-level citation data, a defined content methodology, and documented results from previous clients. Agencies that can't show you your current AI visibility before you sign are not the right fit.

### How do you measure answer engine optimization?

AEO is measured through citation count, mention rate, share of voice (your brand vs. competitors across tracked prompts), average position within AI answers, and prompt-level reporting that shows which queries your brand appears in and which ones it misses. Baseline audits establish where you start; ongoing tracking shows directional progress.

### How long does AEO take to work?

AEO is a compounding strategy. Early indicators — such as new citation sources being indexed and baseline visibility being established — typically appear within 30 days. Meaningful, trackable improvement in citation rates and share of voice generally takes 60 to 90 days, depending on the category, competition level, and starting baseline.
