It is not a general marketing credential. Every module is specific to AEO, and completing it requires demonstrating working knowledge of how answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews evaluate and surface content.

## What the Certification Covers

The AEO Fundamentals curriculum is built around the practical mechanics of getting a business cited by AI assistants. The six completed modules are:

| Module | Focus Area |
| --- | --- |
| Rethinking Visibility in the Age of Answer Engines | How AI assistants have changed where buyers get information and why traditional search rankings no longer tell the full story |
| Establishing Your AEO Foundation | The structural prerequisites a business needs before AI assistants will consistently cite it |
| Planning Content for Search and AI Visibility | How to build a content strategy that serves both Google and AI answer engines simultaneously |
| Optimizing for AI Citations | The specific signals AI platforms use when deciding which sources to pull from |
| Measuring What Matters in AEO | Citation tracking, AI visibility scoring, and the metrics that separate surface activity from real performance |
| AEO Fundamentals Next Steps | Applying the full framework to an active AEO program |

### Why This Curriculum Is Relevant

AEO is not an extension of SEO. The two disciplines share some overlap in content quality standards, but AI assistants use a different set of signals when deciding what to cite. Only [12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10](https://audienceintent.ai/insights), which means strong Google rankings do not transfer automatically to AI visibility.

This curriculum addresses that gap directly. It covers the citation logic that answer engines use, not the ranking logic that search engines use. That distinction is what makes AEO-specific training meaningful rather than redundant.

### What AEO Practitioners Need to Know

Businesses evaluating AEO providers should ask a direct question: does the person managing your AI visibility strategy understand how citation selection actually works?

The answer matters because AEO decisions compound. A content structure built on incorrect assumptions about how AI assistants evaluate sources will not self-correct over time. It will accumulate. Getting the foundation right, which is exactly what this certification curriculum covers, determines whether a business builds genuine AI visibility or just produces content that AI assistants ignore.
