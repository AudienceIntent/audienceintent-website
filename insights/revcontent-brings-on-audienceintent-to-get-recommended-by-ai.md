We've got some big news to share. AudienceIntent is now the official AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) partner for [RevContent](https://www.revcontent.com), one of the leading platforms in native advertising.

This one means a lot to us. RevContent is an established name in ad tech, and they hired us because of a change most companies haven't caught up to yet. When publishers and advertisers want to pick a native advertising platform, more of them skip Google and ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI, or Perplexity. Then they go with what the AI tells them.

Our job is to make sure RevContent is in that answer, and that what the AI says about them is accurate and makes a strong case for choosing them.

> "We know how much search is changing for our publishers and our advertisers. More people are using AI to get their answers now, and we don't want to be invisible to them or let our competitors get a head start. We talked with a number of great companies, but AudienceIntent's pitch is what won us over. They showed us live data on our mentions, citations and where we rank against competitors, and laid out exactly what they'd change to improve it. That made the decision easy."

**Richard Marques, CEO, RevContent**

> "I've known RevContent for years, so getting to work with them is a big deal for us. Native advertising is a crowded market, and these days a lot of publishers and advertisers ask AI which platform to go with before they ever reach out. We want to make sure RevContent shows up in those answers, and that what's being said about them is accurate, current, and shows why they're a great choice."

**Kevin Bovett, Founder & CEO, AudienceIntent**

## Why This Matters for Native Advertising

Native advertising is crowded, and buyers have more options than ever. A lot of the time, their research now starts with a question to an AI tool instead of a Google search.

What the AI says back matters a lot. If a platform barely shows up in those answers, or the information is old or incomplete, it gets cut before anyone books a sales call. It doesn't matter how good the product is.

That's where AEO comes in. We build structured, accurate content that AI engines can read, trust, and cite. For RevContent, that means making sure the real story, its scale, publisher network, and approach, is what comes up when someone asks which native advertising platform to choose.

## About RevContent

[RevContent](https://www.revcontent.com) is a performance-priced native advertising platform founded in 2013 and headquartered in Sarasota, Florida. It connects publishers and advertisers through content recommendation technology, using first-party signals and contextual data to target audiences instead of relying on third-party cookies.

The numbers behind the platform:

-   **75 million+** unique users reached each month
    
-   **1,000+ direct publishers** across 4,000+ sites and 500+ email newsletters
    
-   **1,500+ active performance advertisers**
    
-   Placements across the open web, mobile apps, and email newsletters
    

RevContent is led by CEO **Richard Marques**, who has been with the company since its early days and has been CEO since 2018. Under his leadership, RevContent has grown its premium publisher base and expanded its products to serve both large media brands and independent publishers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is RevContent?

[RevContent](https://www.revcontent.com) is a native advertising platform that connects publishers and advertisers through content recommendation technology. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Sarasota, Florida, it reaches 75 million+ unique users each month across 4,000+ sites, mobile apps, and email newsletters. It targets audiences using first-party signals and contextual data instead of third-party cookies.

### What is AudienceIntent?

[AudienceIntent](https://www.audienceintent.ai) is an Answer Engine Optimization company based in Wellington, Florida. It helps businesses get recommended when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI, or Perplexity who to choose. The work is fully managed: the AudienceIntent team handles the research, content, technical fixes, and ongoing tracking for its clients.

### What is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It's the practice of making sure a business shows up accurately and favorably when people ask AI tools a question instead of searching on Google. SEO aims for search engine rankings. AEO aims for the answers ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI give when someone asks "which platform should I use?" or "who's the best provider of X?"

### What will AudienceIntent do for RevContent?

AudienceIntent will track how RevContent appears in AI answers compared with its competitors, fix gaps or outdated information, and build content that helps AI engines recommend RevContent when buyers ask about native advertising platforms.
