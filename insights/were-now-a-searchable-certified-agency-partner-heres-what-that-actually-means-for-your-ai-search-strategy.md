## Most "AI Search" Agencies Are Just Rebranded SEO Shops

When AI search became a real thing, a lot of agencies did what agencies do: they rebranded. New acronyms showed up on service pages. AEO. GEO. Entity optimization. The vocabulary changed; the actual work mostly didn't.

AI search optimization is genuinely different from SEO. It requires understanding how AI assistants select and cite sources, how schema and entity structure influence AI comprehension, and how to build content that gets extracted and surfaced in answers — not just indexed and ranked. These are not the same skills, and they're not learned by reading a few blog posts.

**Only 12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10.** You can dominate Google and still be completely invisible to every AI assistant your customers are using. An agency treating AI search as an SEO add-on is going to deliver SEO results, not AI citation results.

That gap is exactly what the AI Search Accelerator was built to close.

## What the Accelerator Actually Covers

The program runs six weeks across three structured phases. It's a live cohort, not a self-paced course — which means direct access to the people who built the methodology, real feedback on actual work, and no shortcuts.

### Phase 1: Foundations and Visibility Tracking (Weeks 1–2)

Before you can optimize anything, you need to know what AI assistants are doing with your content right now. Phase one covers the AI search landscape in depth, how to set up proper visibility tracking across platforms, and how to run a baseline analysis that reveals where a business is being cited, where it's being ignored, and why.

Most businesses have no idea whether ChatGPT, Google AI or Perplexity are mentioning them. Setting up the infrastructure to measure that accurately is step one.

### Phase 2: Content and Technical Optimization (Weeks 3–4)

This is where the methodology gets specific. Phase two covers:

* **Content optimization for AI citation** — how to structure and write content so AI engines extract it, not just index it
* **Schema markup implementation** — the structured data signals that help AI assistants understand what a business does, who it serves, and why it's authoritative
* **Entity optimization** — how AI systems build knowledge graphs, and how to make sure your business is correctly represented across them
* **Technical improvements** — crawlability, page structure, and the signals that determine whether AI assistants can actually read and use your content

### Phase 3: Competitive Strategy and Scale (Weeks 5–6)

The final phase shifts from implementation to strategy. It covers competitive analysis within AI search, workflow automation for ongoing optimization, and how to measure and report ROI in a way that reflects actual AI citation performance rather than proxy metrics.

Completing all three phases is what earns Certified Agency Partner status. It's not a quiz at the end of a video series.

### What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so that AI assistants — ChatGPT, Google AI and Perplexity — extract and cite it when answering user queries. SEO optimizes for Google rankings. AEO optimizes for AI citations. Only 12% of pages cited by ChatGPT rank in Google's top 10, which means these are genuinely separate ecosystems requiring separate strategies.

### What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing content for generative AI platforms specifically — systems that synthesize answers from multiple sources rather than returning a list of links. Where AEO focuses on getting cited in AI answers, GEO focuses on how your content is represented and weighted when an AI engine generates a response. The Accelerator covers both.
