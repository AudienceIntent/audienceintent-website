Questions or requests: support@audienceintent.ai

2. Our service

We offer one service: AI Recommended™, a managed Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) service. We work to get your business found, understood and recommended by answer engines such as ChatGPT, Google AI (AI Overviews and AI Mode) and Perplexity.

The service includes making your site open to crawlers, website fixes (schema, sitemap and on-page), customer question research, new and updated articles each month, a weekly visibility report, competitor tracking and a monthly progress call with a Slack channel for questions.

AEO is different from traditional search engine optimization (SEO), although the two work together. Nobody can guarantee that a business will appear in a specific answer. See Section 12.

3. What data we collect

Information you give us

  • Name and business name
  • Email address and, if you choose to share it, phone number
  • Business website, industry and location
  • Anything you send through our booking, onboarding or referral forms
  • Access details you share so we can work on your website, Google Business Profile or analytics

Information collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser, device and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on page and where you came from
  • Clicks, scroll depth and form completions
  • Referral signals from answer engines where we can detect them

Information from other sources

  • Publicly available business information, such as listings, reviews and search results
  • Calendar and booking details when you book a call
  • Answers returned by ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity and similar tools for the prompts we track

4. How we use your data

We use it to:

  • Deliver and improve AI Recommended and our reports
  • Reply to your questions and booking requests
  • Send emails you've agreed to receive
  • Run and report on your campaign
  • Understand how people use our website
  • Meet our legal obligations and protect against fraud or abuse

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use your data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Email

We collect email addresses when you book a call, fill in a form, become a client or subscribe. We use them to reply, confirm bookings, send reports and share updates you've asked for.

Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email support@audienceintent.ai at any time. Transactional emails such as booking confirmations and reports aren't covered by marketing opt-outs. We follow the CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.).

6. Who we share data with

We use these providers to run our business. Each handles data for us under its own privacy policy and, where required, a data processing agreement:

  • Google: Analytics (GA4), Tag Manager, Search Console and Business Profile
  • Anthropic and OpenAI: writing and analysis tools used in our reports and content workflow
  • Searchable: visibility tracking and analytics
  • LeadConnector / HighLevel: CRM, booking, forms and email
  • Slack: client communication
  • Netlify, Cloudflare and GitHub: hosting, infrastructure and website code
  • Stripe: payments

We don't share personal data with anyone for their own marketing. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of AudienceIntent, our clients or the public. If AudienceIntent is acquired or merged, your data may transfer as part of that deal, and this policy will continue to protect it.

7. Data security

  • HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) for all data sent to and from our sites
  • Keys and passwords stored as encrypted environment variables, never in public code
  • Access to client systems limited to the people doing the work
  • Multi-factor authentication on every system holding personal data
  • Regular updates and security patches
  • Client logins and access removed when an engagement ends

No system is 100% secure. If a breach affects your rights, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities within the time the law requires, including 72 hours under GDPR where it applies and the Florida Information Protection Act.

8. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and bookings: up to 3 years, or until you ask us to delete them
  • Client data: for the length of our work together plus 3 years for accounting and legal reasons
  • Website access to client systems: removed within 30 days of the end of an engagement
  • Server logs: up to 90 days
  • Marketing emails: until you unsubscribe

To ask us to delete your data, email support@audienceintent.ai. We reply within 30 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, delete, move or limit the use of your data, object to marketing, or withdraw consent. Email support@audienceintent.ai. We reply within 30 days and may need to confirm who you are.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what we collect and how we use it, to correct it, to limit use of sensitive data, to opt out of its sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising) and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

EU, EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We rely on your consent, our legitimate interest in running our business, or our contract with you. You can complain to your local supervisory authority. We don't have an EU representative but respond to every GDPR request directly.

Florida (FIPA and Florida Digital Bill of Rights)

Under Florida Statute § 501.171 you're entitled to breach notice within 30 days. Where the Florida Digital Bill of Rights applies, you can request access, correction, deletion, portability and opt-out of targeted advertising.

12. AI Recommended™ disclaimer

No one can guarantee that a business will appear in a specific answer from ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity or any other tool. Be wary of anyone who says they can.

What we do promise

  • The work described in your agreement, done properly and in good faith
  • A weekly visibility report from day one, with prompts tracked every day
  • A monthly progress call and a Slack channel for questions
  • Ongoing changes to our approach as these platforms change

What we can't promise

  • That you'll appear in any particular answer or position
  • Specific revenue, leads or sales
  • Results faster than the timeframes we agree at onboarding
  • Protection from changes made by the platforms themselves

Why it takes time

Answer engines need time to find your content, trust it and start recommending you. The first 90 days build the foundation. Ending early limits what's possible and does not entitle you to a refund for work already done.

Platform changes

ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity and others change how they work, often without notice. We adapt as part of the service, but we aren't liable for drops caused by changes outside our control.

13. Results disclaimer

Results on this site, including the Vault Metal, GoldenCrest Metals and Craft Food Tours examples, come from specific clients in specific situations. They aren't a promise of what you'll get. Your results depend on your industry, competition, website, reviews, how quickly changes can be made and other things outside our control. Past results don't guarantee future results. Nothing here is financial, legal, tax or investment advice.
