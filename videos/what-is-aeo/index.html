What Is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization Explained

November 11, 2026·1:38 watch·Transcript included·Video 1 of 18

The short answer

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the work of helping AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity find, understand and recommend your business. When someone asks AI who to hire or where to go, it can return a short list of recommendations. AEO helps your business become one of those names.

Key takeaways

  • An answer engine is any AI tool people ask questions to: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI.
  • AI can answer a full question with a shortlist of businesses. Being named matters, not just being linked.
  • AI names businesses it can find, understand and trust. That's the whole game.

What it looks like

Someone asks AI for a nightclub in Miami. The video shows how a recommendation can name just a few businesses.

Example shown in the video

What's the best nightclub in Miami for a Saturday night?
The example names three venues: Club Space, LIV and Eleven.

An illustration from the video, not a current search result. AI answers can change with the prompt, location, tool and date.

The lesson: a business needs to be easy for AI to find, understand and trust. Strong information and credible mentions help; no placement is guaranteed.

What does AEO stand for?
